# Intro, outro and music-cue brief

You are adding three things to existing exam-prep podcast transcripts. Read the series bible first: production/SERIES.md (format rules). Then read each assigned transcript in full before editing it.

The goal is three passes over each episode's topics, each framed differently, which is a deliberate learning technique: a preview (advance organiser), the body, then retrieval practice followed by a recap.

## 1. Intro preview (new, at the very top of the body, before the cold open)

Four to six short turns, about 120-180 words. Music plays before it, so open straight in.
- SAM welcomes the listener to "Pass the Architect" and names the episode.
- Frame it as what the listener will be able to DO by the end, not a topic list: "By the end you'll be able to spot X, choose between Y and Z, and..." Cover every main segment of the episode, in order, in plain words.
- ALEX adds one line on why it matters for the exam (weight, or the trap it fixes).
- End the intro on the turn that hands to the cold open, and put [sting] at the very end of that turn's line (after any other text).

## 2. Segment stings (transition music)

Put [sting] at the end of the last turn of each major segment, where the conversation moves to a new topic: typically three to five per episode. Never on a line that already ends with [pause]. Not inside a drill. One right before "Five to remember" is good.

## 3. Outro recap (new, after "Five to remember", before the final next-episode tease line)

Six to ten short turns, about 200-300 words.
- Retrieval first: SAM asks the listener to pause and name the topics covered (or the giveaway for each), ending that line with [pause]. Then ALEX has a go out loud.
- Recap second, framed differently from both the intro and "Five to remember": as exam tells, one per main segment. "If a question says or shows X, think Y." Use only facts already in the episode.
- Keep the existing final tease line as the last line of the episode. If the tease currently sits before "Five to remember", move it to the end.

## Hard constraints

- Do not change any existing drill, option, answer key, fact or number. Additions only, apart from moving the tease.
- Every body line starts with "ALEX: " or "SAM: ", one turn per line, blank line between turns, no em dashes, numbers spelled out, write for the ear. Tags allowed: [pause] at the end of a question line, [sting] at the end of a line.
- Raise target_minutes in the front matter by two. Nothing else in the front matter changes.

Edit each file in place. Do not commit. Reply with, per file: number of stings added, intro and outro word counts.
