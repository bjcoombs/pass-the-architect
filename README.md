# Pass the Architect

Study podcasts for the Claude certification exams, and a series on the companies Claude competes with, made with Claude and ElevenLabs:

| Series | Feed |
|---|---|
| CCAR-P (Professional) | https://bjcoombs.github.io/pass-the-architect/ccar-p.xml |
| CCAR-F retake (Foundations) | https://bjcoombs.github.io/pass-the-architect/ccar-f-retake.xml |
| CCDV-F (Developer, Foundations) | https://bjcoombs.github.io/pass-the-architect/ccdv-f.xml |
| CCAO-F (Associate, Foundations) | https://bjcoombs.github.io/pass-the-architect/ccao-f.xml |
| Know the Field (OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, AWS, xAI and open weights) | https://bjcoombs.github.io/pass-the-architect/know-the-field.xml |

To get them all at once, import https://bjcoombs.github.io/pass-the-architect/podcasts.opml. The feeds are unlisted, so subscribe by URL (in Apple Podcasts: Library, then Follow a Show by URL).

These are unofficial study notes, not Anthropic material, and contain no exam items. Know the Field reflects vendor offerings as researched on 14 November 2026; prices and rankings in it date quickly.

## Layout

- `docs/` is the GitHub Pages site: feeds, OPML, covers and chapter JSON, all generated by `production/tools/publish.py`. The feeds point at `feed.xsl` and the OPML at `opml.xsl`, so a browser shows them as pages; podcast apps ignore both.
- `production/` holds everything needed to make or remake the series:
  - `METHODOLOGY.md`: the process, and how to adapt it to another exam.
  - `SERIES.md`, `FOUNDATIONS-RETAKE.md`, `DEVELOPER-FOUNDATIONS.md`, `ASSOCIATE-FOUNDATIONS.md` and `KNOW-THE-FIELD.md`: the series bibles.
  - `transcripts/`, `foundations/transcripts/`, `developer/transcripts/`, `associate/transcripts/` and `field/transcripts/`: the episodes.
  - `field/research/`: the sourced fact sheets behind Know the Field, one per vendor group.
  - `briefs/`: the revision briefs given to the writing agents.
  - `cards/`: per-episode drill data (question, options, correct answer) behind the chapter artwork.
  - `tools/`:
    - `render.py`: ElevenLabs text-to-speech with a cache, chapters with artwork, and a spend cap.
    - `cards.py`: draws the segment, question and answer cards.
    - `publish.py`: copies to iCloud, uploads release assets and writes the feeds.
    - `renumber_retake.py`: makes the spoken episode numbers match the listening order.
  - `music/`: intro, sting and outro.
- The audio lives in GitHub releases, one per series (`ccar-p`, `ccar-f-retake`, `ccdv-f`, `ccao-f`, `know-the-field`), not in git.
