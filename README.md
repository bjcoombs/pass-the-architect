# Pass the Architect

Two study podcasts for the Claude Certified Architect exams, made with Claude and ElevenLabs:

| Series | Feed |
|---|---|
| CCAR-P (Professional) | https://bjcoombs.github.io/pass-the-architect/ccar-p.xml |
| CCAR-F retake (Foundations) | https://bjcoombs.github.io/pass-the-architect/ccar-f-retake.xml |

To get both at once, import https://bjcoombs.github.io/pass-the-architect/podcasts.opml. The feeds are unlisted, so subscribe by URL (in Apple Podcasts: Library, then Follow a Show by URL).

These are unofficial study notes, not Anthropic material, and contain no exam items.

## Layout

- `docs/` is the GitHub Pages site: feeds, OPML, covers and chapter JSON, all generated by `production/tools/publish.py`.
- `production/` holds everything needed to make or remake the series:
  - `METHODOLOGY.md`: the process, and how to adapt it to another exam.
  - `SERIES.md` and `FOUNDATIONS-RETAKE.md`: the series bibles.
  - `transcripts/` and `foundations/transcripts/`: the episodes.
  - `briefs/`: the revision briefs given to the writing agents.
  - `tools/`:
    - `render.py`: ElevenLabs text-to-speech with a cache, chapters and a spend cap.
    - `publish.py`: copies to iCloud, uploads release assets and writes the feeds.
    - `renumber_retake.py`: makes the spoken episode numbers match the listening order.
  - `music/`: intro, sting and outro.
- The audio lives in the GitHub releases `ccar-p` and `ccar-f-retake`, not in git.
